Ethical use of GrimoireLab

The CHAOSS project recognizes that there are ethical and legal challenges when using the metrics and software provided by the CHAOSS community. Ethical challenges exist around protecting community members and empowering them with their personal information. Legal challenges exist around GDPR and similar laws or regulations that protect personal information of community members. Particular challenges may arise in the use that is specific to your context.

GrimoireLab analyzes data that includes personal information about contributors. The enriched indices store names, usernames, email domains, organizations and, for some data sources such as GitHub, locations; the raw indices keep the original data, such as the email addresses in commits. Before running an analysis, check what each data source stores in the GrimoireLab data schema, and decide how you will store, share and publish the results. Contributor identities and affiliations are managed with SortingHat.
